Comments: The readthrough terminator downstream of valS leads to a 3.0 kb monocistronic valS transcript. Based on homology, transcriptional readthrough is expected to occur at the stem-loop upstream of valS if uncharged valine tRNA binds to the T-box motif in the nascent mRNA transcript. The tRNA anticodon binds to the GUC codon in the sequence AAGGUCGCC in the valS leader mRNA.
